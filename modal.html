Explaining the Core Front-end Technologies

What is HTML?

The backbone and structural foundation of any webpage. It tells the browser what content to display on the page (like a skeleton).

What is CSS?

The visual styling and design layer. It makes the webpage look good by controlling the layout, colors, fonts and overall presentation. Without CSS every website would just look like a plain text.

What is Javascript?

The programming language that adds life and functionality. It makes the webpage interactive and dynamic. It allows the page to react to user actions without forcing you to reload the entire screen.
